We started by trying to understand the core frustrations of bird owners when it comes to feeding their pets. Synthesising the most important points and observations (using empathy maps) revealed a few common themes:

<ProblemQuote>
  He's very picky! He will only eat certain fruit and veg... that's chopped in a specific way.
</ProblemQuote>

<ProblemQuote>
  It can be hard to find trustworthy information, especially for the less common species.
</ProblemQuote>

<ProblemQuote>
  A lot of what I do is dictated by the weather... that influences what they have and how much.
</ProblemQuote>

<ProblemQuote>
  I make my own hand blended mixes; I want to know where the ingredients come from.
</ProblemQuote>

## Personas

Based on our interviews, we developed key user personas to guide our design decisions:

<PersonaStepper>
  <Persona name="Mehreen" quote="I just want to give my bird the right food for him, he's very picky.">
    Mehreen has one parrot that lives inside with the family. When she adopted him, he was eating pre-made mix that wasn't particularly organic or healthy, especially for his species. She's since added fresh food to his diet, and faced some challenges with him adapting to change.
    
    <Goals>Be able to give her bird treats without worrying about the potential health impact; Be confident that the feed she provides is right for her bird's species and situation; Make her bird's diet as complete and organic as possible</Goals>
    
    <Frustrations>Her bird is very picky about food, and often rejects new things; Most pre-made mixes aren't suitable for her bird's species, which has specific needs; Difficulty getting her bird to switch to a new feed, as he prefers previous unhealthy ingredients</Frustrations>
  </Persona>
  
  <Persona name="David" quote="My bird deserves the best.">
    David is an experienced bird owner who manages an outdoor aviary with a mixed flock. He spends a significant amount of time researching nutrition and sourcing individual ingredients to create his own custom blends, which can be exhausting and expensive.
    
    <Goals>Feed a balanced, healthy diet tailored to each species in his flock; Easily source high-quality, organic ingredients in bulk without depending on multiple suppliers; Reduce the time and expense spent on quality control</Goals>
    
    <Frustrations>Difficulty finding species-specific feeds in large quantities; Custom mixes from local mills are often too expensive; Inconsistent quality from online seed vendors</Frustrations>
  </Persona>
</PersonaStepper>

## Generating Ideas

<ImageText image="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1618005182384-a83a8bd57fbe?auto=format&fit=crop&q=80&w=1000" alt="Wireframe sketches" position="right">
  With a "no bad ideas" mindset, I sketched some rough solutions addressing persona needs in different ways. Starting loose means I can generate concepts I might not normally consider.
  
  This allowed me to rapidly test the concept of a "build-your-own" feed interface versus a "questionnaire-based recommendation" system.
</ImageText>

<ImageText image="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1517292987719-0369a794ec0f?auto=format&fit=crop&q=80&w=1000" alt="Hi-Fi Designs" position="left">
  After testing the lo-fi concepts with a few target users, the "build-your-own" approach was the clear winner, but users requested a guided mode for beginners.
  
  The final visual design embraces the vibrant, organic nature of the ingredients, using a fresh color palette inspired by nature and custom illustrations to keep the interface friendly and approachable.
</ImageText>

## Final Result

The final product is an iOS app that allows bird owners to either take a quiz to get a recommended feed blend based on their bird's species, age, and environment, or build a custom mix from scratch using a library of over 50 organic ingredients.
